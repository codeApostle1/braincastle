ℹ️ About the app

A little about BrainCastle

BrainCastle is a free learning and quiz platform created by Joel Temitope Ademola, from the Mathematics Department of Kaduna State University.

It helps students turn their study materials into quizzes, practise before examinations and class tests, and share questions with coursemates and other learners. Families and friends can also use it to enjoy quiz games together. BrainCastle is open to everyone: anyone can create an account, make quizzes, share them, and learn — completely free.
